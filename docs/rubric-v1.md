# Rubric v1 — Swedish SPA Negotiation Trainer

## Purpose

Provide a transparent, coachable, and auditable scoring standard for training outcomes.

## Scoring Scale

Each domain uses a 0-100 score.

- **90-100 (Deal-ready):** consistently strong judgment and execution.
- **75-89 (Developing+):** generally strong with occasional misses.
- **60-74 (Developing):** acceptable fundamentals with material gaps.
- **40-59 (Foundational):** recurring errors requiring guided drills.
- **0-39 (At risk):** major misunderstandings or unstructured approach.

## Domains and Anchors

### 1) Legal Accuracy
Measures correctness of legal interpretation and issue spotting.

Strong indicators:
- Correctly identifies clause purpose and legal effect.
- Spots legal inconsistencies and missing protections.
- Distinguishes position preferences from legal requirements.

Common deductions:
- Misstates legal effect of warranty/indemnity language.
- Misses key dependencies (e.g., CP/termination interactions).

### 2) Commercial Judgment
Measures prioritization and value-at-risk reasoning.

Strong indicators:
- Focuses negotiation effort on economically material points.
- Uses sensible fallback positions tied to mandate.

Common deductions:
- Over-indexes on low-impact points.
- Trades high-value protections for minor concessions.

### 3) Negotiation Execution
Measures tactical behavior and concession strategy.

Strong indicators:
- Sequences concessions deliberately.
- Uses principled rationale rather than positional repetition.
- Maintains mandate discipline under pressure.

Common deductions:
- Concedes too early without reciprocal gain.
- Fails to test assumptions or probe opposing rationale.

### 4) Drafting Precision
Measures clarity, coherence, and internal consistency.

Strong indicators:
- Draft text is clear and internally consistent.
- Terms align with defined terms and cross-references.

Common deductions:
- Ambiguous or contradictory drafting.
- Missing dependencies or unresolved drafting collisions.

### 5) Process / Project Management
Measures ability to manage transaction workflow.

Strong indicators:
- Identifies critical path and sequencing constraints.
- Escalates appropriately when risk tolerance changes.

Common deductions:
- Misses timeline dependencies.
- Poor issue-list discipline.

### 6) Client Communication
Measures clarity and usefulness of advice.

Strong indicators:
- Advice is concise, decision-oriented, and actionable.
- Clearly frames options and risk trade-offs.

Common deductions:
- Overly technical advice with unclear recommendation.
- Fails to connect legal points to business outcomes.

## Weighted MVP Composite

For MVP, composite score can use:
- Legal Accuracy: 30%
- Commercial Judgment: 25%
- Negotiation Execution: 25%
- Drafting Precision: 20%

## Feedback Requirements (per session)

1. Numeric domain scores.
2. Top 3 strengths.
3. Top 3 improvement priorities.
4. Recommended drills mapped to specific deductions.
5. “Why this matters” note per priority.

## Calibration Protocol

- Weekly benchmark session scoring against a golden set.
- Supervisor review of outlier scores (>15-point drift from benchmark).
- Update anchor examples monthly.
